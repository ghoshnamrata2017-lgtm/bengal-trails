Jalpaiguri Division

1. Darjeeling

Himalayan Ridge

Famed world-wide for muscatel tea gardens, Mt. Kanchenjunga dawn panoramas, and UNESCO heritage narrow-gauge rail.

Key Tourist Places:
Tiger Hill Batasia Loop Peace Pagoda Ghoom Monastery Singalila Park
restaurant Craft & Taste: First Flush Muscatel Tea, Thukpa & Churpi cheese
Best Season Oct – May
Jalpaiguri Division

2. Kalimpong

Sub-Himalayan

Tranquil highland retreat known for colonial stone cottages, orchid nurseries, Buddhist monasteries, and quiet pine forests of Lava.

Key Tourist Places:
Deolo Hill Cactus Nursery Morgan House Lava & Rishyap Lolegaon Canopy
restaurant Craft & Taste: Kalimpong Hand-crafted Lollipops & Cheddar Cheese
Best Season Sep – Jun
Jalpaiguri Division

3. Jalpaiguri

Dooars Forest

Lush Dooars foothills dotted with emerald tea plantations, dense sal timber tracts, and wildlife watching on the Murti riverbank.

Key Tourist Places:
Gorumara Park Murti Riverbed Samsing & Rocky Island Suntalekhola Chapramari Reserve
restaurant Craft & Taste: Dooars CTC Green Tea & Freshwater Boroli Fish
Best Season Oct – Apr
Jalpaiguri Division

4. Alipurduar

Sanctuary Corridor

Eastern gateway of Dooars, blessed with one-horned rhinoceros habitats, the historical ruins of Buxa Fort, and Jayanti river valley.

Key Tourist Places:
Jaldapara Rhino Park Buxa Fort & Tiger Reserve Jayanti Riverbed Chilapata Elephant Forest
restaurant Craft & Taste: Rava Tribe Handloom Shawls & Organic Wild Honey
Best Season Nov – Apr
Jalpaiguri Division

5. Cooch Behar

Royal Heritage

The grand seat of the Koch Maharajas, celebrated for its Renaissance-style Victor Jubilee Palace, terracotta temples, and serene dighis.

Key Tourist Places:
Cooch Behar Palace Madan Mohan Temple Rasikbil Birds Sagardighi Lake
restaurant Craft & Taste: Sitalpati Reed Mats & Traditional Koch Jolpan
Best Season Oct – Mar
Malda Division

6. Uttar Dinajpur

Avian Sanctuary

Home to Raiganj Kulik Bird Sanctuary—one of Asia's largest open-billed stork heronries—flanked by agrarian rivers and historical artifacts.

Key Tourist Places:
Kulik Bird Sanctuary Karnajora Museum Burhana Temple Sap Nikla Forest
restaurant Craft & Taste: Tulaipanji Aromatic Rice & Dhokra clay lamps
Best Season Oct – Mar
Malda Division

7. Dakshin Dinajpur

Ancient Archaeological

An archaeologist's treasure vault featuring the Mauryan-to-Pala epoch ruins of Bangarh, massive historic reservoirs, and lush bird sanctuaries.

Key Tourist Places:
Bangarh Excavation Mahipaldighi Lake Sarongbari Eco Forest Balurghat Museum
restaurant Craft & Taste: Mukhomukhi Wooden Masks & Radhatilak Rice
Best Season Nov – Feb
Malda Division

8. Malda

Medieval Capital

The majestic sultanate twin capitals of Gour and Pandua, famed for massive stone portals, Adina Mosque, and Fazli mango orchards.

Key Tourist Places:
Adina Mosque Baro Sona Masjid Dakhil Darwaza Jagjivanpur Buddhist Vihara
restaurant Craft & Taste: Malda Fazli Mangoes, Kansat Sweets & Mulberry Raw Silk
Best Season Oct – Mar
Malda Division

9. Murshidabad

Nawabi Splendor

Former seat of the Bengal Nawabs along the Bhagirathi river, rich with neoclassical palaces, Katra mosque, and Murshidabad silk weaving.

Key Tourist Places:
Hazarduari Palace Katra Mosque Kathgola Gardens Motijheel Lake Wasif Manzil
restaurant Craft & Taste: Murshidabad Baluchari Silk, Chhana Boroboti Sweet
Best Season Oct – Mar
Presidency Division

10. Nadia

Sacred Vaishnavite

Spiritual heart of Gaudiya Vaishnavism and Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu; renowned for Mayapur ISKCON, Krishnanagar clay dolls, and Santipuri handloom.

Key Tourist Places:
Mayapur ISKCON Nabadwip Dham Bethuadahari Sanctuary Krishnanagar Rajbari
restaurant Craft & Taste: Sarpuria & Sarbhaja sweets, Krishnanagar Clay Dolls
Best Season Year-round
Burdwan Division

11. Birbhum

Red Soil & Culture

Red-soiled sanctuary of Nobel Laureate Rabindranath Tagore's ashram university, mystical Baul song traditions, and Tantric shaktipeeths.

Key Tourist Places:
Visva-Bharati University Sonajhuri Saturday Haat Tarapith Temple Bakreshwar Hot Springs
restaurant Craft & Taste: Kantha Embroidery Sarees, Leather Shantiniketan Bags
Best Season Oct – Mar
Burdwan Division

12. Purba Bardhaman

Granary & Temples

The rice bowl of Bengal, graced by the concentric 108 Shiva Temples of Kalna, regal Curzon Gate, and GI-tagged royal sweet delicacies.

Key Tourist Places:
108 Shiva Temples (Kalna) Curzon Gate Burdwan Sarbamangala Temple Krishnasayar Lake Park
restaurant Craft & Taste: Bardhaman Sitabhog & Mihidana, Tangail Handloom
Best Season Oct – Mar
Burdwan Division

13. Paschim Bardhaman

Lakes & Heritage

Industrial titan cradling scenic waters: serene Maithon reservoir boating, rebel poet Kazi Nazrul Islam's village, and Ma Kalyaneshwari shrine.

Key Tourist Places:
Maithon Dam & Island Kalyaneshwari Temple Churulia Nazrul Academy Durgapur Barrage
restaurant Craft & Taste: Langcha sweets of Shaktigarh & Dokra trinkets

Medinipur Division

14. Bankura

Terracotta Paradise

Land of red soil, terracotta temples of Bishnupur, Dokra metal castings, the massive Mukutmanipur earthen dam, and Susunia hill springs.

Key Tourist Places:
Bishnupur Temples Mukutmanipur Dam Susunia Hill Biharinath Peak Bikna Dokra Village

Medinipur Division

15. Purulia

Rugged Hills & Chhau

Enchanting Ajodhya hill range with cascading waterfalls, vermilion Palash blossoms in spring, volcanic granite hillocks, and acrobatic Chhau dance.

Key Tourist Places:
Ajodhya Hills & Bamni Falls Joychandi Pahar Charida Chhau Mask Hub Baranti & Murardi Lake
restaurant Craft & Taste: Vibrant Charida Chhau Masks, Mahua flower snacks
Best Season Oct – Mar
Medinipur Division

16. Jhargram

Junglemahal Woods

Heart of Bengal's forested Junglemahal, famed for royal palaces, ancient medicinal groves of Chilkigarh, and pristine river canyons of Ghagra.

Key Tourist Places:
Jhargram Palace Chilkigarh Kanak Durga Ghagra Waterfalls Belpahari Hill Forests
restaurant Craft & Taste: Sal-leaf art & Tribal Sabai Grass woven baskets
Best Season Oct – Mar
Medinipur Division

17. Paschim Medinipur

Stone Fortresses

Ancient stone architecture of Kurumbera Fort, the freedom struggle sanctuaries of Karnagarh, and scroll painting artisans of Naya (Pingla).

Key Tourist Places:
Kurumbera Fort (Gaganeswar) Gopegarh Eco Park Karnagarh Temple Pingla Patachitra Hub
restaurant Craft & Taste: Pingla Natural Dye Scroll Paintings & Babui Grass mats

Medinipur Division

18. Purba Medinipur

Coastal Beaches

Golden sands of Bay of Bengal from Digha and Mandarmani to the ancient maritime silk port of Tamralipta (Tamluk) and Shankarpur harbour.

Key Tourist Places:
Digha Beach & Aquarium Mandarmani Red Crab Sands Tajpur Pine Grove Shankarpur Harbor Tamluk Bargabhima
restaurant Craft & Taste: Fresh Fried Pomfret & Sea Prawns, Madur Mat weaving
Best Season Year-round
Burdwan Division

19. Hooghly

European River Enclaves

The historic European river settlements of the Portuguese (Bandel), French (Chandannagar), and Dutch alongside the terracotta towers of Bansberia.

Key Tourist Places:
Bandel Basilica Church Hooghly Imambara Chandannagar Strand Hanseswari Temple (Bansberia) Tarakeswar Shiva
restaurant Craft & Taste: Chandannagar Lighting Art & Jalbhora Talsas Sandesh
Best Season Year-round
Presidency Division

20. Howrah

Twin City & Botany

Twin city to Kolkata across the cantilever Howrah Bridge; custodian of the worldwide Ramakrishna Math at Belur and the historic Great Banyan Tree.

Key Tourist Places:
Belur Math Headquarters Great Banyan Botanic Garden Howrah Bridge Vistas Gadiara Confluence
restaurant Craft & Taste: Zari Embroidery of Panchla & Hilsa fish fry
Best Season Year-round
Presidency Division

21. Kolkata

State Capital

City of Joy: 300 years of intellectual fervor, white marble Victoria Memorial, Dakshineswar Kali shrine, trams, and UNESCO Durga Puja festival.

Key Tourist Places:
Victoria Memorial Dakshineswar Temple Indian Museum Prinsep Ghat Kumartuli Idol Hub
restaurant Craft & Taste: Kolkata Biryani, Nolen Gur Rosogolla & Mishti Doi

Presidency Division

22. North 24 Parganas

Border & Mangroves

International river frontier at Taki along Ichamati, deer sanctuaries at Parmadan Forest, and the northern mangrove gateway at Dhamakhali.

Key Tourist Places:
Taki Ichamati Riverfront Dhamakhali Boat Point Parmadan Wildlife Sanctuary Chakla Loknath Temple
restaurant Craft & Taste: Taki Rajbari Gamcha & Fresh Chingri Malai Curry

Sundarban National Park Gangasagar Kapil Muni Henry's Island Beach Sajnekhali Watchtower Diamond Harbour

When to Visit Which Region

Autumn & Winter (Oct - Feb) Peak Season

Ideal for Sundarbans mangrove cruises, Darjeeling Himalayan viewpoints, Bishnupur temple tours, and Durga Puja & Poush Mela festivities.

Spring Flush (Mar - May) Blooming Season

Best for Rhododendron blooming in Singalila, Palash flower bursts in Purulia, first flush tea harvesting, and coastal sea breezes in Mandarmani.

Monsoon Magic (Jun - Sep) Eco Off-Beat

Spectacular for Shantiniketan rain retreats, lush rain-washed tea estates in Dooars, Jhapan snake-boat festivities, and Khichuri culinary trails.

Wildlife Safari Sanctuary Calendar Advisory

All North Bengal national parks (Jaldapara, Gorumara, Buxa) remain closed for animal breeding from June 16 to September 15 annually. Plan accordingly.
